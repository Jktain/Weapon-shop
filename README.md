3D idle tycoon game.
Medieval weapons shop. There are 4 spots for crafting weapons: swords, shields, bows and axes, as well as 2 spots for resource extraction - wood and metal. Currency - gold. Weapons are crafted with a certain period, if there are materials for crafting. At first, only a building for making swords is available. With a certain period, NPCs spawn with a random desired item (from the list of those currently being made) in one of the 4 spawn locations and go to the store building, where they line up and wait for the desired item to be made. The inventory is presented in the form of a panel, which shows a list of items, their quantity and the selling price of one copy. I also made a save/load progress function.
